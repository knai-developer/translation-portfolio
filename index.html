About Me

Two Languages, One Message

Knai Translations is an English↔Urdu translation and localisation service. We convert content between English and Urdu without losing its meaning: the same warmth, the same formality, the same intent — just in the language the reader actually thinks in.

My work spans app and website localisation, payment and fintech copy, UI strings, security alerts, long-form editorial and reusable bilingual glossaries. I use AI translation agents for speed and consistency, then proofread every line by hand — because machine-translated Urdu often reads like Urdu, but doesn't feel like it.

EN → UR Translation

English content turned into pure, natural Urdu — short, friendly app copy that reads like it was written in Urdu first.

UR → EN Translation

Urdu translated back into clear, natural English — idiomatic, correct and ready for international audiences.

App & UI Localisation

Interface strings, notifications and micro-copy localised with right-to-left Urdu support in mind.
